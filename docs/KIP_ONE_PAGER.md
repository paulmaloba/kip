## What is KIP?

**KIP is a FREE AI-powered tool that recommends personalized business ideas for Zambian entrepreneurs.**

Instead of generic advice like "start a shop," KIP analyzes:
- ✅ Your exact budget (K1,000 to millions)
- ✅ Your location (down to your specific neighborhood)
- ✅ What businesses already exist nearby
- ✅ Market gaps nobody has filled
- ✅ Current economic conditions
- ✅ Your skills and available time

Then gives you **specific, data-driven recommendations** with success probabilities.
